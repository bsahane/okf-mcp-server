# Acme Travel Policy (2026)

Applies to all employees travelling on company business from 1 January 2026.

## Booking

All flights and hotels must be booked through the Acme travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class needs written approval from a Vice President.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 USD in most cities. In London, New York and Tokyo the limit is 260 USD per night. Stays above the limit need approval from the traveller's cost-centre owner.

## Meals

Meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to a per diem of 75 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy are approved by the Finance Controller.
