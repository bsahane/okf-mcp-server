# Submitting an expense claim

## Steps

1. Photograph every receipt.
2. Create a claim in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense.
3. Attach receipts and select the cost centre.
4. Submit; your line manager approves claims up to 1,000 USD, and the cost-centre owner approves larger claims.

## Payment

Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run.
